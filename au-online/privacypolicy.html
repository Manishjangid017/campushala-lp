01

Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide to Us

When you fill out an enquiry form, request a callback, download a brochure, or contact us, we may collect:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone / WhatsApp number
  • City / location
  • Educational qualification and course/program of interest
  • Any other information you voluntarily provide in forms or during counselling calls

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

When you visit our Website, certain information may be collected automatically through cookies and similar technologies, including:

  • IP address

We may receive information about you from third-party advertising platforms (such as Google Ads and Meta/Facebook Ads) when you interact with our ads, in accordance with those platforms' own privacy policies.

02

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your enquiries and provide counselling regarding courses, universities, and admissions
  • Connect you with our partner universities/institutions for admission guidance
  • Send you relevant information about courses, programs, and offers via email, phone, or WhatsApp
  • Improve our Website, Services, and user experience
  • Measure and improve the effectiveness of our advertising campaigns
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Our Website uses cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance user experience and analyze site traffic. This includes:

  • Google Analytics: To understand how visitors use our Website

We use Google Ads and other third-party advertising services to promote our Services. These third parties may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to our Website or other websites. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings and the Network Advertising Initiative opt-out page.

How We Share Your Information

We may share your information with:

  • Partner universities/institutions: To facilitate your admission enquiry and process, with your consent
  • Service providers: Third parties who help us operate our Website, send communications, or process data (e.g., CRM providers, email/SMS/WhatsApp service providers), under confidentiality obligations

We retain your personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected, including to provide our Services, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.
