OFFICIAL POLICY

Privacy Policy

01

Introduction

Thank you for visiting the Amity University Online website. We are committed to protecting your privacy. As a general rule, we do not collect any personal information about you unless you voluntarily provide it.

We do not sell, share, or transfer your personal information to third parties.

02

Non-Personal Information We Collect

If you browse our website without submitting any personal details, our system may automatically collect certain general information about your visit.

During your visit, we may record:

  • Your internet domain, such as xyz.com, xyz.net, or yourcollege.edu
  • The type and version of your web browser
  • Your operating system, such as Windows, macOS, or Unix
  • The date and time of your visit, along with the pages you access
  • The website you visited prior to arriving at our site, where applicable

This information is used solely for statistical and analytical purposes to improve the usability, functionality, and performance of our website. It does not personally identify you.

03

OTP Verification for Payments

For secure transactions, we may use One-Time Password (OTP) authentication.

When making a payment, you may receive an OTP via SMS on your registered mobile number to verify and complete the transaction securely.

04

Cookies

We use cookies on certain pages of our website to enhance your browsing experience.

Cookies are small files stored on your device that help maintain your session while navigating the website. These cookies do not collect personal information.

You may choose to accept or decline cookies through your browser settings. For added privacy, we recommend closing your browser after completing your session.

05

Information from Emails

If you contact us via email, we may use the information you provide, including your email address, to respond to your inquiry.

Please note that email communication may not always be secure. Avoid sharing sensitive information unnecessarily through email.

06

Consent & Authorization

By registering with Amity University Online, you authorize us and our associates to contact you via email, SMS, WhatsApp, or phone calls between 9:00 a.m. and 9:00 p.m. regarding updates, services, programs, courses, and related information.

This consent applies even if your number is registered under DND / DNDC / NCPR services and remains valid until your account is deactivated.

07

Information Gathered Through Interactive Forms

Certain pages of our website may contain interactive forms that allow you to voluntarily provide personal information such as your name, email address, mobile number, or other relevant details.

This may occur when you:

  • Register for counselling sessions, tests, quizzes, seminars, workshops, or training programs offered by Amity Online
  • Download admission forms or related documents
  • Submit an enquiry regarding a program or course
  • Request information about admissions or academic offerings

The information you provide through these forms is used for its intended purpose and handled in accordance with our applicable privacy practices.

08

Links to External Sites and Partners

This Privacy Policy outlines the privacy practices applicable to the Amity Online website. However, our website may contain links to third-party websites or platforms.

Once you leave the Amity Online website, you may be accessing platforms that are not under our control.

External websites may use their own cookies, collect data, or request personal information. The privacy practices described in this policy do not apply to such third-party websites.

We recommend reviewing the privacy policy of any external website you visit, particularly before sharing personal information.

09

User Authorization

By using Amity University Online services and/or registering on the platform, you consent to being contacted by Amity University Online, including its representatives, affiliates, and business partners, through:

  • Email
  • Phone calls
  • SMS
  • WhatsApp

Such communications may relate to the programs or courses you have selected, admission-related updates, or relevant information about them.

You acknowledge and agree that such communication may occur even if your contact details are registered under DND, DNC, or NCPR services. This consent will remain valid until your account is deactivated, either by you or by us.

10

Internship / Job Opportunities

Eligibility for internships or job opportunities will be determined based on merit and evaluated on a case-by-case basis.

Participation in or eligibility for any internship or job opportunity is subject to the applicable requirements, criteria, and terms of the respective opportunity.

Privacy Commitment

We value your trust and are committed to handling the information you provide responsibly and in accordance with applicable privacy requirements.
