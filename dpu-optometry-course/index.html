PROGRAM

Bachelor of Optometry
(B. Optom.)

  • Correction of refractive errors and prescription of glasses
  • Detection of eye-related systemic and neurological diseases
  • Fitting of contact lenses and low-vision aids
  • Diagnosis and treatment of eye movement disorders, including squint
  • Primary eye care in schools, communities, rural areas, and industries
  • Counselling patients with low vision, colour blindness, and hereditary vision defects
  • Assessment of eye health and timely referral to specialists

Duration:

5 Years (4 years academic training + 1 year compulsory internship)

Eligibility:

12th PCB/PCM with 50% marks, followed by interview & counselling
